About

Kiran Acharya

I write in the spaces between what happened and what I wished had happened. Some feelings arrive too large for conversation, so they wait here instead — folded into lines, kept a little safer on the page than they ever were in my chest. This is not a diary and not quite a confession. It is somewhere quieter than both.
